Ukraine · Lead Story

Ukraine says Kerch Bridge span collapses in second day of strikes

Road arch falls into the Kerch Strait and traffic is halted. Kyiv calls it retaliation for Russian attacks on Ukrainian infrastructure, including two bridges in Kyiv. The Russian-installed Crimea governor reports two killed.

Middle East

U.S. jets hit IRGC missile sites after drone attack on Bahrain base

An IRGC drone targeted U.S. radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base; it was intercepted, with no casualties or damage. Third Iranian attack on Bahrain in five days.

Technology

Nvidia posts record $115.8bn quarter, up 103%

Profit hits $65bn; next-quarter outlook about $130bn. Board will not bring back Jensen Huang as CEO after his 12 November exit.

Health

U.S. loses measles-free status after 26 years

PAHO says the U.S., Mexico and Bolivia lose elimination status as measles returns. CDC counts 5,412 U.S. cases in 2026, over twice 2025’s 2,289.

East Asia

Suspected Russian jets enter Japanese airspace

Two aircraft near Okinoshima, Fukuoka, about 04:07–04:12. Japan scrambles fighters and lodges a strong protest; first breach since 23 Sep 2024.

Diplomacy

Trilateral talks Thursday with Xi Jinping and Putin in Shenzhen; agenda: the Iran and Ukraine wars, nuclear issues, trade and AI.

Yashar party’s Kahana replaces Likud’s Amir Ohana, a Netanyahu ally. Netanyahu stays on as prime minister for now.
